Privacy policy

Euclid Elements, Books I-VI · updated 25 November 2026

The app collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not contact any server of ours.

The propositions you have reviewed are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud key-value storage so that your other devices can show them. Apple handles that storage under its own privacy policy; we cannot see it.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no personal or payment information.
