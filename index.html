Frequently asked

What is free? Book I, propositions 1 to 12. The one-time purchase Euclid I–VI Full opens the other 161.

I bought it on another device. Open About → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

My reviewed marks don’t appear on my iPad. Both devices must be signed in to the same iCloud account; marks arrive within a few minutes.

Reset all progress (About) clears the reviewed marks on all your devices. It does not affect the purchase.

What does “A is to B as C is to D” mean? That A has to B the same ratio as C has to D; today we would write A/B = C/D. Many older editions write it A : B :: C : D, with “:” for “is to” and “::” for “as”. Terms such as alternately, componendo and ex aequali are explained in the app under Definitions and principles (the book icon) and in About → Reading the demonstrations.
